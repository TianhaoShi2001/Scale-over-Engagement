# Synthetic data

These tables are generated by `core/make_synthetic.py` from `core/analysis.yaml`.

The seed is fixed (`seed` in the config). Rerunning the pipeline rewrites the same files.

What is aligned with the manuscript:

- Column roles match the matching covariates and the time-series outcomes in Methods.
- AIGC-adopting creators produce more videos, with a much smaller gap in aggregate valid-view and full-view counts.
- Interactions with platform-labeled AIGC have lower early-retention, valid-view, and full-view rates.
- Platform-labeled AIGC videos have lower cumulative exposure and a more compressed exposure lifecycle.
- Population-feedback consumers have a lower platform-labeled AIGC exposure share, and the individual-minus-population gap remains across 1, 2, 4, and 8 weeks.
- The daily panel has a negative lagged association from s_t^a to M_t, a level association of R_t with S_t and E_t, and a small long-run elasticity of ln(e_t) with respect to ln(s_t^a).

The sample is smaller than the manuscript extract. The daily panel has 180 days. Upload date is stored as `upload_week`. Holiday and campaign flags stand in for those controls. Estimates from this sample are not the manuscript estimates.
